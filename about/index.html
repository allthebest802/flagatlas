About FlagAtlas

FlagAtlas is an educational resource about the world's flags, countries and territories.

We created FlagAtlas to make learning about flags accessible, visual and fun — whether you're a student, teacher, parent, geography enthusiast or simply someone who enjoys flags.

Our aim is simple: help people learn about the world one flag at a time.

What you'll find on FlagAtlas

🌍 Country Flag Database

Explore country flags with information about symbolism, history, geography and national identity.
🏝️ Territories & Regions

Discover flags from territories, regions and constituent countries around the world.
📜 Flag History

Find out how flags have changed and what historical events influenced their designs.
📚 Flag Guides

Learn about colours, stars, moons, crosses, symbols and the stories behind flag designs.
⚡ Flag Quizzes

Test your knowledge and see how many countries you can recognise.
🧠 Memory Games

Match flags and build recognition and recall while playing.
🎨 Colouring Pages

Free printable flag colouring pages for children, classrooms, families and flag fans.
🧭 Flag Explorer & Games

Explore countries, play games and discover interactive geography resources in one place.

Educational resources

FlagAtlas is designed to be useful for learning as well as entertainment. Students and teachers can use the site when studying geography, countries, maps, history and global cultures.

The resources are also intended to work for parents, homeschooling, classroom activities, quiz fans and anyone who simply wants to learn more about the world.

No prior knowledge is needed. You can start with a familiar flag, explore its story, and then follow links to discover countries and flags you may not know.

Who creates FlagAtlas?

FlagAtlas was created and is maintained by an independent UK-based creator with an interest in geography, history, flags and making useful things on the web.

The site started from a simple idea: flags are a surprisingly good way of learning about the world.

A flag can tell you something about a country's history, its people, its politics, its geography and the events that shaped it. But finding that information isn't always straightforward, particularly for students and younger learners.

So FlagAtlas was built to bring that information together — combining flags, country information, history, interactive tools and printable resources in a format that's easy to explore.

It is an ongoing project, and the site continues to grow with new country pages, games, guides and educational resources.

Why did we build FlagAtlas?

There are plenty of websites where you can find a picture of a country's flag.

We wanted to go a little further.

Why does the flag look like that?

What do the colours mean?

Where did the symbols come from?

Has the flag changed over time?

And perhaps most importantly:

What can a flag teach us about the country behind it?

That's what FlagAtlas is about.

A free resource

FlagAtlas is intended to be free to use. You don't need an account to explore the country pages, learn about flags, play the quizzes or use the printable resources.

The project is continually being developed, with new content and tools added over time.
