Non-Rectangular National Flags

Nepal is the world's only current non-rectangular national flag. Discover its double-pennon shape, symbols and constitutional construction.

Nepal — the unique national flag

Nepal's flag consists of two joined triangular pennons rather than a rectangle. It is the only current sovereign national flag with this non-quadrilateral outline. citeturn0search0turn0search12

Two pennons instead of a rectangle

The unusual outline reflects a long South Asian tradition of triangular banners. Nepal retained this distinctive form while most other modern states adopted rectangular national flags.

The moon and sun

The upper pennon carries a crescent moon and the lower pennon carries a sun. These symbols give the flag a particularly recognisable appearance and connect the design with Nepal's older traditions.

A flag defined by geometry

Nepal's constitution gives detailed instructions for constructing the flag. Its shape is therefore not simply an artistic suggestion: the geometry is formally specified. citeturn0search7

Why Nepal stands alone

Switzerland and Vatican City are square, but a square is still a four-sided rectangle. Nepal goes further by having a genuinely non-rectangular national outline. citeturn0search11
