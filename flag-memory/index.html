A free flag memory game

Flag Memory is a free online matching game where you flip the cards to find pairs of identical world flags. It draws from the flags of every country — all 195 nations and more — so each game is a fresh random selection and boards rarely repeat. It's a simple, satisfying way to get to know the flags of the world, from familiar ones like France, Japan and Brazil to trickier flags such as South Korea, Kiribati and Kyrgyzstan. There's no account and nothing to download, and it works on phones, tablets and computers.

How to play

Every card starts face-down. Tap one to reveal its flag, then tap a second to try to find its match. Find the two matching flags and the pair stays face-up; miss, and the cards flip back — so you'll need to remember where each flag was. Clear the whole board to finish, and try to do it in as few attempts and as little time as possible to earn a higher score.

Learn world flags by playing

Matching games are a natural way to build visual memory, and flags are perfect for it. Because you see each flag several times while hunting for its pair, the shapes, colours and emblems start to stick — which makes Flag Memory a fun, low-pressure way for children and adults to learn to recognise the flags of the world. Pair it with the Flag Speed Quiz to test what you've learned, or the flag colouring pages for younger players.

Four difficulty levels

Choose the size that suits you. Easy is a 4×4 grid with 8 flag pairs — great for children and quick games. Medium (4×6) and Hard (6×6) step things up with 12 and 18 pairs. Expert is a full 8×8 board with 32 pairs to test even seasoned flag-spotters. Your best score is saved on your device for each level, so you can keep trying to beat it.

Frequently asked questions

What is Flag Memory?

It's a free flag matching (concentration) game: flip cards two at a time to find pairs of identical world flags until the whole board is cleared.

Is it free to play?

Yes — completely free, with no account, no downloads and no ads. It runs in your browser on any device.

Does it help you learn flags?

Yes. Seeing each flag repeatedly as you search for its match helps you remember its colours and design, so it's a genuinely useful way to learn to recognise world flags.

What difficulty levels are there?

Four — Easy (4×4), Medium (4×6), Hard (6×6) and Expert (8×8), from 8 pairs up to 32.
