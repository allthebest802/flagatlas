Jordan Flag

Black, white, and green represent the three Arab dynasties. Red stands for the Hashemite Dynasty. The white star has seven points representing faith, humanity, national spirit, humility, virtue, social justice, and aspirations.

Capital Amman
Continent Asia
Population 10.3 million

Flag symbolism

Black, white, and green represent the three Arab dynasties. Red stands for the Hashemite Dynasty. The white star has seven points representing faith, humanity, national spirit, humility, virtue, social justice, and aspirations.
