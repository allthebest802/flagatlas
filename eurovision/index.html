📖 A little history

The facts that don't change — handy all year round.

🗓️ Born in 1956

The first contest was held in Lugano, Switzerland in 1956 with just seven countries. It's now the longest-running annual TV music competition in the world.

🏆 Most wins

Ireland and Sweden share the record with seven victories each. The UK, France, the Netherlands and Luxembourg follow on five.

🌍 52 nations, and counting

52 countries have taken part at least once. Around 40 compete each year, with a record 43 in 2008, 2011 and 2018.

✈️ Not just Europe

Israel, Cyprus, Armenia, Georgia and Azerbaijan take part, and Australia has competed every year since 2015. Morocco appeared just once, in 1980.

💶 The "Big Five"

France, Germany, Italy, Spain and the United Kingdom skip the semi-finals and go straight to the final, as the contest's largest financial backers.

🚫 One cancellation

In 70 years the contest has been cancelled only once — in 2020, because of the COVID-19 pandemic.
