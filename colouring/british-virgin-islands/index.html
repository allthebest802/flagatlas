🌍 Flag facts
🕯️
The woman is St Ursula. The eleven lamps come from an old legend about her and her followers.
👀
The motto Vigilate is Latin for "be watchful".
🏝️
The territory is made up of around 60 islands in the Caribbean Sea.
💵
Even though it is British, the territory uses the US dollar.
