🌍 Flag facts
🌅
The circle is often seen as the sun setting over the sea, and the white as the ice.
🗣️
In Greenlandic the flag is called Erfalasorput, meaning "our flag".
🗓️
It was designed by Thue Christiansen and adopted in 1985.
🧊
Greenland is the largest island in the world, and part of the Kingdom of Denmark.
