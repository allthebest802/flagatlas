🌍 Flag facts
🪷
The lotus flower is the symbol of Macau.
🌉
Under the flower are a bridge and the water around Macau.
🗓️
The flag was adopted in 1999, when Macau became part of China.
🇵🇹
Before 1999, Macau was run by Portugal for more than 400 years.
