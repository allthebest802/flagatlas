🌍 Flag facts
🦁
Tanzania has the Serengeti — home to the world's largest wildlife migration of wildebeest and zebra.
🏔️
Tanzania is home to Mount Kilimanjaro — the highest mountain in Africa at 5,895 metres.
🌍
Tanzania was formed in 1964 from the union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar — which is how it got its name.
⬛
Green represents the land, black the people, yellow the mineral wealth, and blue the Indian Ocean and lakes.
