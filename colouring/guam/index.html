🌍 Flag facts
🌴
The coconut palm tree stands for strength, because it can grow in poor soil and survive storms.
⛵
The canoe is a proa, a fast sailing boat built by the Chamorro people.
🟥
The flag was designed in 1917, and the red border was added in 1948.
🌅
Guam is called the place "where America's day begins", because it is so far west across the Pacific.
