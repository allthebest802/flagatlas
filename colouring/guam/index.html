🌍 Flag facts
🇬🇺 Guam is an unincorporated territory of the United States in the western Pacific Ocean.
🔵 The flag has a blue field with a narrow red border.
🏝️ The central seal shows features associated with Guam, including the island's landscape and a coconut palm.
🌊 Guam is the largest and southernmost of the Mariana Islands.
