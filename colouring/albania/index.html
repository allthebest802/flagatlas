🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The double-headed eagle comes from the seal of Gjergj Kastrioti Skanderbeg, Albania's 15th-century national hero.
🏴
Albanians call their country Shqipëria — often translated as "Land of the Eagles".
🎨
Take your time with the eagle — its feathery outline is the trickiest part. Sharp pencils help!
🏕️
Albania is dotted with over 170,000 dome-shaped concrete bunkers — some are now cafes, museums and even homes.
