🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The double-headed eagle comes from the seal of Skanderbeg, Albania's 15th-century national hero.
🏔️
Albanians call their country Shqipëria, which is often translated as "Land of the Eagles".
✏️
Take your time with the eagle. Its feathery outline is the trickiest part, so sharp pencils help!
🏠
Albania is dotted with about 170,000 small concrete bunkers built in the 1970s and 80s. Some are now cafes and museums.
