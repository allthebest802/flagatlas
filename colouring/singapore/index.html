🌍 Flag facts
🌙
The white crescent moon stands for a young nation on the rise. Singapore became an independent country quite recently, in 1965.
⭐
The five stars stand for Singapore's five ideals: democracy, peace, progress, justice and equality.
🎨
Red stands for friendship between all people, and white stands for purity and doing what is right.
🏙️
Singapore is a tiny island country in South-East Asia, but it's one of the busiest and richest in the world. It's famous for its gardens, tall buildings and a giant hotel with a boat-shaped roof!
