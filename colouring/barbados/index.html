🌍 Flag facts
🔱
The trident is deliberately broken — its snapped shaft stands for Barbados breaking away from its colonial past.
🌊
The trident belongs to Neptune, god of the sea — the three prongs stand for government of, for and by the people.
🐟
Barbados is the land of the flying fish — they really do glide above the waves around the island.
🎤
Superstar Rihanna was born in Barbados — she's an official National Hero of the country!
