🌍 Flag facts
🏜️
Western Sahara lies on the Atlantic coast of north-west Africa, and most of it is desert.
🎨
The flag uses the Pan-Arab colours: black, white, green and red.
🌙
A crescent and star appear on many flags across North Africa and the Middle East.
🗺️
Western Sahara's status is disputed, and the United Nations lists it as a non-self-governing territory.
