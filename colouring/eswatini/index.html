Eswatini Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The Nguni shield, two spears and the staff with its tassels are all included. Colour the grey parts of the shield black.

🌍 Flag facts
🛡️
The flag has a Nguni shield lying across two spears and a fighting staff. The black and white shield shows black and white people living together in peace, and it stands for protection of the country.
🎨
The colours have meanings: red for battles of the past, yellow for the country's mineral riches, and blue for peace.
👑
The blue tassels hanging from the staff are called injobo. They are made from feathers and are worn by the king on special occasions.
🌍
Eswatini used to be called Swaziland. It changed its name in 2018. It's one of the smallest countries in Africa and is ruled by a king.
