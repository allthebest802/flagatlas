Cambodia Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The temple is white on the real flag, so you can leave it plain — or take your time and colour in its towers and details.

🌍 Flag facts
🛕
The building is Angkor Wat, a huge temple built over 900 years ago. It's the largest religious monument in the world.
🏳️
Cambodia is one of only a few countries with a building on its flag — and its flag has shown Angkor Wat, on and off, since 1850.
🎨
The colours have meanings: blue for the king, red for the nation, and white for religion.
🌏
Angkor Wat is so important to Cambodia that it appears on the country's money and is the nation's proudest symbol.
