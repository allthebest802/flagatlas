Kazakhstan Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The sun, the eagle and the pattern on the left are all one gold colour, so the many lines inside them are just details — colour them all gold, or leave the fine lines white.

🌍 Flag facts
☀️
The golden sun has 32 rays shaped like grains of wheat, standing for a rich harvest and a bright future. Below it soars a steppe eagle, a symbol of freedom and power.
🎨
The sky-blue background stands for the vast open skies over Kazakhstan and for peace, while the gold stands for the sun and wealth.
🐎
The pattern down the left is a traditional Kazakh design. Kazakhstan has a long history of nomads on horseback roaming the wide grasslands, called the steppe.
🌏
Kazakhstan is the largest landlocked country in the world — it doesn't touch any ocean but is bigger than all of Western Europe. Spaceships also launch from here!
