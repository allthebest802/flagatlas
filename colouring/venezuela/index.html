🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The eight stars stand for the eight provinces that signed Venezuela's declaration of independence in 1811, plus one added later for a province that joined the fight.
🎨
Yellow stands for the country's wealth and sunshine, blue for the sea separating Venezuela from Spain, and red for the blood shed for independence.
🏔️
Venezuela is a country in South America with the world's tallest waterfall, Angel Falls, which drops over 979 metres — that's taller than 3 Eiffel Towers stacked up!
🛢️
Venezuela has some of the largest oil reserves in the world, and its capital city, Caracas, sits in a valley surrounded by mountains near the Caribbean coast.
