🌍 Flag facts
🔄
Paraguay's flag is the only national flag in the world with a different design on each side! The front shows the national coat of arms (a star), and the back shows the treasury seal (a lion).
⭐
The gold star on the front is surrounded by a wreath of palm and olive branches, standing for victory and peace. It's called the 'May Star' for Paraguay's independence in May 1811.
🎨
The red, white and blue colours were inspired by the flag of France and stand for independence, peace and liberty.
🌊
Paraguay is a small country in the middle of South America with no coastline. Instead it has huge rivers — the Paraguay and Paraná rivers — that boats use to reach the sea.
