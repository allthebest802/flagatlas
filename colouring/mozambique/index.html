🌍 Flag facts
⭐
Mozambique's flag is the only national flag in the world with a picture of a modern rifle (an AK-47) on it, crossed with a hoe for farming.
📖
The open book stands for education, the hoe for the country's farmers, and the star for hope and international friendship.
🎨
The colours each have a meaning: green for the land, black for Africa, yellow for the country's minerals, and red for the struggle for independence.
🏖️
Mozambique is a large country on the south-east coast of Africa, with a very long Indian Ocean coastline full of golden beaches, coral reefs and palm trees.
