Uzbekistan Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. In the top corner is a white crescent moon with twelve small white stars arranged in a triangle.

🌍 Flag facts
🌙
The white crescent moon is a symbol of Islam and of Uzbekistan becoming a new independent nation.
⭐
The twelve stars stand for the twelve months of the year and the twelve constellations of the zodiac.
🎨
Light blue honours Timur (Tamerlane), a great Uzbek leader from history, white stands for peace, and green for nature and fertility. The thin red lines stand for the life force in every living thing.
🕌
Uzbekistan is a country in Central Asia famous for its ancient Silk Road cities like Samarkand and Bukhara, filled with beautiful blue-tiled mosques and domes.
