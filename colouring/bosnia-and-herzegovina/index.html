🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The stars at the top and bottom are cut in half on purpose — it suggests the row of stars goes on forever.
📐
The three points of the triangle stand for the country's three main peoples — and its roughly triangular shape on the map.
🇪🇺
The blue, yellow and stars were chosen to match the colours of Europe — the flag was adopted in 1998.
🌉
The beautiful Stari Most (Old Bridge) in Mostar is over 400 years old — divers leap from it into the river below!
