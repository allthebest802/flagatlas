🌍 Flag facts
🚢
The shield shows the wreck of the Sea Venture, an English ship that ran aground on Bermuda in 1609.
🟥
Bermuda is the only British Overseas Territory to use a red background on its flag. The others use blue.
🦁
The red lion holding the shield is a symbol of England.
🌊
Bermuda lies in the North Atlantic Ocean, about 1,000 km off the east coast of the USA.
