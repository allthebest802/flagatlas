Equatorial Guinea Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The emblem in the middle has a silk-cotton tree with six stars above it — colour the tree grey-brown and leave the stars white.

🌍 Flag facts
🌳
The tree in the middle is a silk-cotton tree (also called a God tree). An important treaty was once signed under one of these trees.
⭐
The six stars above the tree stand for the mainland part of the country and its five islands.
🎨
The colours mean: green for the forests, blue for the sea that links the islands to the mainland, white for peace and red for the fight for independence.
🗣️
Equatorial Guinea is the only country in Africa where Spanish is an official language, a reminder of its history. It sits on the west coast, near the equator.
