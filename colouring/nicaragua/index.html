🌍 Flag facts
🌋
The triangle in the middle shows five volcanoes, standing for the five countries of Central America that were once joined together.
🌈
Above the volcanoes is a rainbow and a red cap of liberty, symbols of peace and freedom. Around the triangle are the words "República de Nicaragua, América Central".
🎨
Nicaragua's two blue stripes stand for the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea on either side of the country, and the white stands for peace and the land.
🦜
Nicaragua is the largest country in Central America, full of volcanoes, lakes and rainforests. It's home to colourful parrots, monkeys and turtles.
