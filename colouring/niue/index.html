🌍 Flag facts
☀️
The yellow stands for Niue's bright sunshine and its warm friendship with New Zealand.
⭐
The large star in the blue circle stands for Niue. The four small stars are the Southern Cross.
🏝️
Niue is one of the world's largest coral islands.
🗓️
The flag was adopted in 1975.
