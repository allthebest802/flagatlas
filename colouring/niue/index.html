🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The five yellow stars are unique to Niue’s flag.
🔵
The big star sits on a blue disc in the centre.
🇬🇧
The Union Jack shows Niue’s ties to Britain and New Zealand.
🌊
Niue is a small island nation in the South Pacific.
