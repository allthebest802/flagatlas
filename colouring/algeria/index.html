🌍 Flag facts
🌙
The red crescent and star sit right across the middle, so the background behind them changes from green to white.
🗺️
Algeria is the largest country in Africa, and the tenth largest in the world.
🏜️
More than four-fifths of Algeria is covered by the Sahara, the biggest hot desert on Earth.
🎨
The crescent and star are symbols of Islam. Green is often said to stand for nature and white for peace.
