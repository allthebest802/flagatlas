🌍 Flag facts
🐗
The golden circle is a curved boar's tusk, a traditional symbol of wealth and status on the islands of Vanuatu.
🌿
Inside the tusk are two crossed namele leaves (a type of fern). They stand for peace, and are also used in local ceremonies and even appear on Vanuatu's parliament building.
🔠
The black Y-shape is called a pall. Its shape represents the way local church ministers led people out of the darkness of conflict and into the light.
🌋
Vanuatu is a chain of more than 80 islands in the South Pacific, known for its volcanoes, coral reefs, and being one of the happiest countries in the world according to some surveys.
