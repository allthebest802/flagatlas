🌍 Flag facts
🐗
The golden curl is a boar's tusk. On the islands, curled pig tusks are a traditional sign of wealth and are worn as pendants.
🌿
Inside the tusk are two crossed namele leaves from a palm-like plant called a cycad. They stand for peace, and their 39 little leaflets stood for the 39 members of the first parliament.
🎨
The golden Y-shape is said to show the shape of the island chain and the light of Christianity. Black stands for the people, red for blood and green for the rich land.
🌋
Vanuatu is a chain of about 80 islands in the South Pacific, known for its active volcanoes and coral reefs.
