Uganda Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. In the middle is a white circle with Uganda's national bird, the grey crowned crane, with one leg raised. The PDF has a second page with the crane drawn extra large.

🌍 Flag facts
🐦
The bird is a grey crowned crane, Uganda's national bird. It has a spiky golden crest on its head and is known for its graceful dancing.
🎨
Black stands for the people of Africa, yellow for the African sunshine, and red for the brotherhood that links all Africans.
🚩
The crane was on Uganda's badge before independence in 1962 and kept its place on the new flag. Its raised leg is said to show the country moving forward.
🦍
Uganda is in East Africa and is often called the 'Pearl of Africa'. It is home to Lake Victoria, the source of the River Nile, and about half of the world's mountain gorillas.
