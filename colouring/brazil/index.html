Brazil Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. All 27 stars are shown in their real positions, and the motto ORDEM E PROGRESSO follows the curve of the white band, just like the real flag.

🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The globe has 27 stars — one for each of Brazil's 26 states plus its capital district. They're arranged as the night sky looked over Rio de Janeiro on 15 November 1889.
🌌
One star sits above the white band and the rest below. That single star, called Spica, stands for the state of Pará in the north of the country.
📜
The white band carries the words "Ordem e Progresso" — Portuguese for "Order and Progress".
🌎
Brazil is the largest country in South America and the fifth-largest in the world, and most of the Amazon rainforest is inside it.
