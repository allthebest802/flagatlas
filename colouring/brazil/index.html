🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The globe has 27 stars — one for each of Brazil's 26 states plus its capital district. They're arranged exactly as the night sky looked over Rio de Janeiro on 15 November 1889.
🌌
One star sits above the white band and the rest below. That single star stands for the state of Pará, which lies north of the equator.
📜
The white band carries the words "Ordem e Progresso" — Portuguese for "Order and Progress".
🌎
Brazil is the largest country in South America and the fifth-largest in the world, and most of the Amazon rainforest is inside it.
