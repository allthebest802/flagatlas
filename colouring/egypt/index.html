Egypt Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The eagle in the middle is all one gold colour, so you can colour it in gold — the lines inside it are just its feathers and details.

🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The gold bird is the Eagle of Saladin, named after a famous ruler from long ago. It is a symbol used across the Arab world.
🎨
The three colours tell a story: red for the country's past struggles, white for a peaceful future, and black for the end of hard times under earlier rulers.
🛡️
On the eagle's chest is a small shield with the flag's colours, and below it a scroll that reads 'Arab Republic of Egypt' in Arabic.
🏛️
Egypt is home to the Pyramids of Giza and the Great Sphinx, built over 4,500 years ago — the only one of the ancient wonders of the world still standing.
