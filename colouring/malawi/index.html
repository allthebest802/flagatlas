🌍 Flag facts
☀️
The rising sun stands for the dawn of hope and freedom for Malawi and for the whole of Africa. It has 31 rays.
🎨
The colours each have a meaning: black for the people of Africa, red for the blood shed for freedom, and green for Malawi's evergreen nature and land.
🌊
Malawi is home to Lake Malawi, one of the largest lakes in Africa. It's nicknamed the 'Lake of Stars' and is full of colourful little fish called cichlids.
❤️
Malawi is known as the 'Warm Heart of Africa' because its people are famous for being so friendly and welcoming.
