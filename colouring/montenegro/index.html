Montenegro Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The eagle is mostly one gold colour, so the many lines inside it are just its feather details — colour it all gold, or leave the fine lines white. Only the little shield on its chest uses other colours.

🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The golden bird is a double-headed eagle — an ancient symbol with two heads looking both east and west. It holds an orb and a sceptre, signs of a ruler's power.
🦁
On the eagle's chest is a small shield showing a golden lion, a symbol of bravery and of the church. The eagle comes from the old royal family of Montenegro.
🎨
The red field with a gold border is a very royal, regal design — Montenegro was once a kingdom ruled by princes and kings.
⛰️
The name Montenegro means 'Black Mountain'. It's a small, rugged country on the Adriatic Sea with dramatic mountains, deep canyons and beautiful old coastal towns.
