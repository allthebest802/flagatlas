🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The white bird is a double-headed eagle — an eagle with two heads looking in opposite directions. It's a very old royal symbol used across eastern Europe for hundreds of years.
🛡️
On the eagle's chest is a red shield with a white cross and four shapes called firesteels (they were once used to make sparks). They stand for a Serbian saying about unity.
🎨
The red, blue and white stripes are the traditional Slavic colours, shared by several countries in the region like Russia and Slovakia.
🏀
Serbia is a country in south-east Europe. It's famous for being brilliant at basketball, tennis and water polo — the tennis champion Novak Djokovic is from Serbia.
