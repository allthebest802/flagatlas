🌍 Flag facts
🏰
The three towers on the flag are real! They stand on the three peaks of Mount Titano and are called Guaita, Cesta and Montale. You can still visit them today.
🕊️
The word on the scroll is 'LIBERTAS', which means liberty (freedom). The white stands for peace and the clouds, and the blue for the sky.
🗺️
San Marino is one of the smallest countries in the world — you could drive across it in about 20 minutes! It sits completely surrounded by Italy.
📜
San Marino says it is the oldest republic in the world, founded way back in the year 301 — over 1,700 years ago — by a stonemason named Marinus.
