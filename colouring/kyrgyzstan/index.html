Kyrgyzstan Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The sun and the criss-cross pattern inside it are all one gold colour, so the many lines are details — colour it all gold, or leave the fine lines white.

🌍 Flag facts
☀️
The golden sun has 40 rays, one for each of the 40 ancient Kyrgyz tribes that were united into one nation by the hero Manas.
🏠
The criss-cross pattern in the middle is a tunduk — the round wooden crown at the top of a yurt, the traditional round tent the Kyrgyz people live in. You're looking up at it from inside!
🎨
The red background stands for bravery. Red was said to be the colour of the flag of the hero Manas.
🏔️
Kyrgyzstan is a very mountainous country in Central Asia — most of it is covered by the peaks of the Tian Shan mountains, and horses are a huge part of life there.
