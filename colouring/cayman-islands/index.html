🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The three green stars stand for the three islands: Grand Cayman, Cayman Brac and Little Cayman.
🐢
The green turtle on top recalls the turtles that once drew sailors to the islands.
🍍
The pineapple stands for the islands' link with Jamaica.
📜
The motto reads "He hath founded it upon the seas".
