Argentina Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The sun is drawn with all 32 rays, but its face is left out to keep it easy to colour.

🌍 Flag facts
☀️
The sun is the Sun of May, named after the May Revolution of 1810 that started Argentina's journey to independence. On the real flag it has a human face.
🔢
Count the rays and you'll find 32 — sixteen straight ones and sixteen wavy ones, taking it in turns all the way round.
🎨
The flag was created by Manuel Belgrano in 1812, and the sun was added six years later in 1818.
🏔️
Aconcagua, in Argentina, is the highest mountain anywhere outside Asia at nearly 7,000 metres.
