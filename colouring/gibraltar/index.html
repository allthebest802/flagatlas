🌍 Flag facts
🏰
The castle and key come from the coat of arms given to Gibraltar by Queen Isabella of Spain in 1502.
🗝️
The key shows Gibraltar as the "key to the Mediterranean", guarding the way in from the Atlantic.
🐒
The Rock of Gibraltar is home to Barbary macaques, the only wild monkeys in Europe.
🇬🇧
Gibraltar is the only British Overseas Territory whose flag has no Union Jack.
