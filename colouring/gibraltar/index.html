🌍 Flag facts
🏰
The red castle has three towers.
🔑
The gold key stands for the "key to the Mediterranean."
👑
The design was granted by Queen Isabella of Castile in 1502.
🐒
Gibraltar’s Barbary macaques are the only wild monkeys in Europe.
