🌍 Flag facts
👑
The golden crown stands for the Tajik people's sovereignty and their long, proud history. Crowns like this appear on ancient artefacts found in Tajikistan.
⭐
The seven stars arranged in an arc above the crown represent the seven regions of Tajikistan, unified as one nation, along with the seven days of creation in local folklore.
🎨
Red stands for the unity and sovereignty of the nation, white for cotton (a major Tajik crop) and purity, and green for agriculture and the country's mountain valleys.
🏔️
Tajikistan is a mountainous country in Central Asia — over 90% of the land is mountains! It's home to the Pamir range, sometimes called the 'Roof of the World'.
