Dominica Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. Dominica’s flag has a very detailed parrot in the middle, so it is drawn here as a simple shape — you can colour it purple and green like the real bird.

🌍 Flag facts
🦜
The bird is a Sisserou parrot, found only on Dominica. It's the national bird, and Dominica is the only country in the world with a parrot on its flag.
⭐
The ten green stars around the circle stand for Dominica's ten parishes (the areas the island is divided into).
✝️
The three-colour cross stands for the Christian faith, and its three colours — yellow, black and white — stand for the island's people, soil and rivers.
🌋
Dominica is called the 'Nature Island'. It's covered in rainforest and has a boiling lake heated by a volcano — the second-largest hot lake in the world.
