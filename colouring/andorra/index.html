🌍 Flag facts
🛡️
The shield is split into four parts, one for each of Andorra's historic rulers — a bishop's mitre and crook, the three stripes of Foix, the four stripes of Catalonia, and two cows for Béarn. The pictures are left out here to keep it colourable.
🤝
Andorra has two heads of state at the same time — the President of France and a bishop from Spain. It's the only country in the world run this way.
🏔️
Andorra is a tiny country high in the Pyrenees mountains between France and Spain, and its capital is the highest capital city in Europe.
📜
The real coat of arms has a scroll underneath reading "VIRTVS VNITA FORTIOR" — Latin for "strength united is stronger".
