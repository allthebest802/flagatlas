South Korea Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. Each black bar is drawn as an outline, so colour every bar in black.

🌍 Flag facts
☯️
The circle in the middle is called the Taegeuk. Its red and blue swirls stand for balance between opposites, like day and night.
⚫
The four groups of black bars are called trigrams. They stand for heaven, earth, water and fire.
🎮
South Korea is the home of K-pop music and has a huge video gaming culture. It is one of the world's most high-tech countries.
🍜
South Koreans eat more instant noodles per person than almost anyone else in the world!
