White

🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The three six-pointed stars represent the three main peoples of Burundi — the Hutu, Tutsi and Twa.
🥁
Burundi is famous for its royal drummers — the drumming tradition is UNESCO-protected cultural heritage.
🌍
Burundi is one of the smallest and most densely populated countries in Africa.
🎨
Red stands for the struggle for independence, green for hope, and white for peace.
