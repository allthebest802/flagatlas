🌍 Flag facts
🧵
The red and white stripe on the left is based on a traditional Belarusian woven pattern, like the ones on a rushnik — a decorated cloth towel.
🌲
The name Belarus comes from White Rus, an old name for the region. The country is famous for its vast ancient forests, including the Białowieża Forest, home to European bison.
📅
This flag was adopted in 1995 and is very similar to the Soviet-era flag, with the communist symbols removed.
🎨
Green represents Belarus's forests and fields, and red represents the blood of defenders of the motherland.
