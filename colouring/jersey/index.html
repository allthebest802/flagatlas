🌍 Flag facts
🇯🇪 Jersey is a Crown Dependency in the English Channel, close to the coast of Normandy.
✝️ The flag has a red saltire on a white field.
👑 A golden crown and crest are displayed in the centre of the flag.
🏝️ Jersey is the largest of the Channel Islands.
