🌍 Flag facts
🦁
The badge has three gold lions on a red shield, the old arms of the Dukes of Normandy and English kings.
👑
The badge and crown were added to the flag in 1981.
🏝️
Jersey is the largest of the Channel Islands, about 22 km from the coast of France.
🇬🇧
Jersey is a Crown Dependency, with its own government, and is not part of the United Kingdom.
