🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The ring of 15 white stars stands for the 15 islands that make up the Cook Islands.
🌊
The Cook Islands are a group of islands in the South Pacific, in free association with New Zealand.
🇬🇧
The Union Jack in the corner shows the islands' historic links to Britain and the Commonwealth.
🌴
Cook Islanders are New Zealand citizens and use the New Zealand dollar.
