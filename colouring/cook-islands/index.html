🌍 Flag facts
⭐
The 15 white stars stand for the 15 islands of the Cook Islands.
⭕
The stars are in a circle, to show the islands are equal and united.
🇳🇿
The Cook Islands govern themselves in free association with New Zealand.
🗓️
The flag was adopted in 1979.
