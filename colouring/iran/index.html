Iran Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The red shape in the middle is Iran's national emblem. The real flag also has a band of small writing along the edges of the green and red stripes, which is left off here to keep it simple.

🌍 Flag facts
🌷
The red emblem in the middle is made of four curved shapes and a sword. Many people say it looks like a tulip, an old symbol in Iranian culture.
🎨
The colours have meanings: green for growth and nature, white for peace, and red for bravery.
🐱
Iran is where the fluffy Persian cat comes from, and it was once the heart of the mighty Persian Empire.
🕌
Iran is famous for beautiful tiled mosques, ancient palaces like Persepolis, and colourful handmade Persian carpets.
