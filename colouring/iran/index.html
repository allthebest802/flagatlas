Iran Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The red shape in the middle is Iran's national emblem. The rows of little white squiggles along the edges of the green and red stripes are the words Allahu Akbar ("God is great") written 22 times in a blocky script. Leave them white!

🌍 Flag facts
🌷
The red emblem in the middle is made of four curved shapes and a sword. It is a stylised form of the word "Allah", and many people say it also looks like a tulip, an old symbol in Iranian culture.
🎨
The colours have meanings: green for growth and nature, white for peace, and red for bravery.
🐱
Iran is where the fluffy Persian cat comes from, and it was once the heart of the mighty Persian Empire.
🕌
Iran is famous for beautiful tiled mosques, ancient palaces like Persepolis, and colourful handmade Persian carpets.
