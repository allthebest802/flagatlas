🌍 Flag facts
🌿
The gold emblem is an olive wreath with an olive branch inside it. It stands for peace, and it also appeared on Eritrea's first flag, back in 1952.
🎨
The colours each have a meaning: red for the blood shed in the fight for freedom, green for farming and the land, and blue for the sea.
🌊
Eritrea sits on the Red Sea in the Horn of Africa, with a very long coastline and hundreds of islands.
🗓️
Eritrea is a young country — it became independent in 1993 after a long struggle, and adopted this flag in 1995.
