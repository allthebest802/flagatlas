Kiribati Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The bird and the sun are both gold, so the lines inside the bird are feather details — colour it all gold, or leave the fine lines white.

🌍 Flag facts
🐦
The golden bird is a frigatebird, a seabird with a huge wingspan. It stands for power, freedom, and the ocean-going skill of the Kiribati people.
☀️
The rising sun has 17 rays, one for each of Kiribati's 16 Gilbert Islands plus Banaba Island. It sits right on the horizon of the sea.
🌊
The three white and blue wavy bands stand for the Pacific Ocean and the three groups of islands that make up the country.
🏝️
Kiribati is made of 33 islands spread across a huge area of ocean. It's one of the few countries to sit in all four hemispheres at once!
