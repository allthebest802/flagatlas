🌍 Flag facts
☀️
The golden sun has eight rays, one for each of the first eight provinces that rose up in the fight for freedom from Spain in 1896.
⭐
The three stars stand for the three main island groups of the Philippines: Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.
🔄
The Philippines flag is special: in times of war it is flown upside-down, with the red band on top instead of the blue!
🏝️
The Philippines is a country in South-East Asia made up of over 7,600 islands, with beautiful beaches, volcanoes and rainforests full of unique animals.
