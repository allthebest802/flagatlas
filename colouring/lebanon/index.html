🌍 Flag facts
🌲
The tree is the famous Lebanon cedar, a mighty evergreen that can live for over a thousand years. It's the national symbol of Lebanon.
🎨
The two red stripes stand for the blood of those who defended the country, and the white stripe stands for peace and Lebanon's snowy mountains.
📜
Cedar trees have been prized for thousands of years. The ancient Phoenicians, who came from Lebanon, built ships and traded cedar wood all around the Mediterranean.
🏔️
The name 'Lebanon' comes from a word meaning 'white', because of the snow that caps its mountains — you can even ski and swim in the sea on the same day!
