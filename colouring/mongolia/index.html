Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The tall symbol on the left is all one gold colour, so the many shapes inside it are just details — colour it all gold, or leave the fine lines white.

🌍 Flag facts
🔥
The golden symbol is called the Soyombo. At the very top is a flame standing for growth and family, with the sun and moon below it — very old symbols of the Mongolian people.
☯️
In the middle is a shape like a yin-yang (two fish), standing for balance. The triangles and bars around it stand for spears defending the country and for honesty and justice.
🎨
The red stripes stand for progress and strength, and the blue stripe in the middle stands for the vast eternal blue sky over Mongolia.
🐎
Mongolia is a land of wide open grasslands called the steppe. Many people still live as nomads in round tents called gers, and are famous as expert horse riders.
