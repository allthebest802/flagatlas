🌍 Flag facts
👑
The gold crown was added to the flag in 1937. It stands for the royal family — Liechtenstein is a principality, ruled by a prince.
🎨
The blue stands for the sky and the red for the glowing fires lit in people's homes in the evening.
🤝
The crown was added after Liechtenstein discovered its flag looked exactly the same as Haiti's at the 1936 Olympics! The crown made it unique.
🏔️
Liechtenstein is a tiny country in the Alps between Switzerland and Austria. It's so small you can walk across it in a few hours, and it has no airport.
