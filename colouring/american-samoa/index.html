🌍 Flag facts
🇦🇸 American Samoa is a United States territory in the South Pacific.
🦅 The flag features a prominent bald eagle, representing the relationship with the United States.
⭐ The eagle holds traditional items associated with Samoan authority and culture.
🌊 The islands are part of the Samoan Islands in Polynesia.
