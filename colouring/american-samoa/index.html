🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The bald eagle is the national bird of the United States.
🪃
The eagle holds a war club and a fly whisk, Samoan symbols of a chief's power and wisdom.
🗓️
The flag was adopted in 1960.
🌏
American Samoa is the southernmost territory of the United States, in the South Pacific.
