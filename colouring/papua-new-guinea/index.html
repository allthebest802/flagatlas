Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The flag is split by a diagonal line: red in the top corner with a golden bird, black in the bottom corner with white stars. The bird is all one gold colour, so the lines inside are just its details.

🌍 Flag facts
🐦
The golden bird is a Raggiana bird-of-paradise, the national bird of Papua New Guinea. It is famous for its beautiful, colourful feathers used in traditional dances.
⭐
The five white stars form the Southern Cross, a group of stars you can see in the night sky south of the equator. Several countries in the southern half of the world show it on their flags.
🎨
Red and black are traditional colours used in Papua New Guinean art and tribal culture. The flag was designed by a 15-year-old schoolgirl, Susan Karike, in 1971.
🌴
Papua New Guinea sits just north of Australia. It has thick rainforests, high mountains and hundreds of islands — and more than 800 different languages are spoken there, more than any other country!
