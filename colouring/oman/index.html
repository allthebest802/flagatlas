Oman Flag Colouring Page

Print the outline below and colour it in using the numbered guide. The numbers on the flag match the colour swatches — so you always know exactly which colour goes where. The emblem at the top of the red stripe is all one white colour, so the many fine lines inside it are just its details — colour it white, or leave the lines and colour around it.

🌍 Flag facts
🗡️
The white emblem is the national emblem of Oman: a curved dagger called a khanjar crossed with two swords. The khanjar is worn on special occasions and is a proud symbol of Oman.
🎨
The red stands for battles fought in Oman's history, the white for peace, and the green for the fertile land and the green hills of the Jebel Akhdar mountains.
🏜️
Oman is a country in the Middle East, on the Arabian Peninsula. It has golden deserts, rugged mountains and a long coastline on the Arabian Sea.
🐪
Oman is famous for frankincense — a sweet-smelling tree resin traded for thousands of years — and for its beautiful forts, camels and traditional wooden sailing boats called dhows.
