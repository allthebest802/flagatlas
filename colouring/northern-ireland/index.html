🌍 Flag facts
🚩
This is the Ulster Banner, the flag of the former Government of Northern Ireland from 1953 to 1972.
🇬🇧
Since 1972 it has had no official status. The official flag in Northern Ireland is the Union Flag.
✋
The red hand is the Red Hand of Ulster, an old symbol of the province of Ulster.
⭐
The six-pointed star is often said to stand for the six counties of Northern Ireland.
