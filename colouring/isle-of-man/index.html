🌍 Flag facts
🦵
The symbol is a triskelion: three armoured legs joined at the thigh.
🎲
The island's Latin motto means "whichever way you throw it, it will stand".
🌊
The Isle of Man sits in the Irish Sea, between Great Britain and Ireland.
🏛️
Its parliament, Tynwald, is said to be the oldest continuous parliament in the world.
