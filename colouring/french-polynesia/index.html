🌍 Flag facts
🛶
The emblem shows a Polynesian canoe sailing over the sun and the sea.
👥
The five figures on the canoe stand for the five island groups of French Polynesia.
🏝️
French Polynesia has over 100 islands, including Tahiti and Bora Bora.
🗓️
The flag was adopted in 1984.
