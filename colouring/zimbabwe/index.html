🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The golden Zimbabwe Bird is based on soapstone bird carvings found at Great Zimbabwe, an ancient stone city that the country is named after.
⭐
Behind the bird is a red star, often said to stand for the nation's hopes for the future. The white triangle stands for peace.
🎨
Green stands for farming and the land, gold for mineral wealth, red for the blood shed in the struggle for independence, and black for the people of Zimbabwe.
🐘
Zimbabwe is in southern Africa. It shares the mighty Victoria Falls with Zambia, and its national parks are home to huge herds of elephants.
