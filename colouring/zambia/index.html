🌍 Flag facts
🦅
The orange bird is an African fish eagle, flying free above the country. It stands for the people's freedom and their ability to rise above the nation's problems.
🎨
Green stands for Zambia's farms and forests, red for the struggle for freedom, black for the people of Zambia, and orange for the country's mineral wealth, especially copper.
🚩
Zambia's flag is unusual because the stripes and eagle sit in the corner rather than in the centre or along one edge, like many other flags.
💧
Zambia is a country in southern Africa, home to Victoria Falls (shared with Zimbabwe) and the mighty Zambezi River, one of the longest rivers in Africa.
