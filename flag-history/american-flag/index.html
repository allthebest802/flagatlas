The American Flag

From thirteen revolutionary colonies to fifty stars — the story of the Stars and Stripes.

United States flag
France

Before the Stars and Stripes

Before independence, the American colonies used a variety of British and colonial flags.

The American Revolution

The Revolution created a need for symbols representing the new political movement and distinguishing the colonies from Britain.

The thirteen stripes

The thirteen stripes represent the original thirteen colonies.

1777 — the first Stars and Stripes

On 14 June 1777, the Continental Congress adopted a national flag with thirteen stars and thirteen stripes.

Why the stars changed

Stars were added as new states joined the Union, making the flag a visual record of American expansion.

The 50-star flag

Hawaii became a state in 1959. The 50-star design was officially adopted on 4 July 1960 and remains the US flag.

What do the colours mean?

Later traditions give symbolic meanings to red, white and blue, but the original 1777 resolution did not assign a detailed meaning to each colour.

The flag and American identity

The Stars and Stripes has become a powerful symbol of the United States at government buildings, ceremonies, schools and sporting events.
