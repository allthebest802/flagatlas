How to get better at flag quizzes

The best way to improve your flag knowledge is repetition with feedback — which is exactly what this quiz provides. A few tips: learn flags by their dominant colours first, then by their emblems and shapes. Tricolour flags (three vertical or horizontal stripes) are common — focus on the colour order and which nation uses which combination. Nordic cross flags (Scandinavia) all share the same cross design but in different colours. Crescent and star flags are common across the Middle East and Central Asia.
