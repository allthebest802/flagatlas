Flag of Curaçao

A blue field with a yellow stripe and two white stars.

The flag of Curaçao has a blue field crossed by a yellow stripe below the centre, with two white stars of different sizes in the upper hoist. Curaçao is a constituent country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands, an island in the southern Caribbean just off the coast of Venezuela.

About the Curaçao flag

The flag was adopted on 2 July 1984. The blue field stands for the sea and sky around Curaçao, and the yellow stripe for the bright sunshine that bathes the island. The two white stars represent Curaçao and its little sister island, Klein Curaçao; their five points are also read as the five continents from which the island's people have come. Curaçao's capital, Willemstad, with its colourful Dutch colonial architecture, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Frequently asked questions

What does the flag of Curaçao look like?

A blue field with a yellow stripe below the centre and two white stars of different sizes in the top-left.

What do the two stars mean?

They represent Curaçao and its little neighbour Klein Curaçao; their five points also stand for the five continents the island's people come from.

Is Curaçao part of the Netherlands?

Curaçao is a constituent country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands, with its own government, flag and anthem — not a separate UN country, which is why it sits here under Territories & Regions.

When was the flag adopted?

On 2 July 1984, marked each year as Curaçao's Flag Day.
