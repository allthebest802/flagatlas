Flag of Martinique

A red, green and black design representing Martinique, a French Caribbean island known as the Island of Flowers.

The current Martinique flag uses red, green and black in a distinctive three-part design. A red triangle fills the left side, while the right side is divided horizontally into green above and black below. This is the same design used in the free FlagAtlas colouring page.

The Martinique flag

The red, green and black flag was adopted in 2023, according to the FlagAtlas colouring resource. It is one of the newest national or territorial flag designs in the world. The design is simple enough for children to colour while still being distinctive and easy to recognise.

Frequently asked questions

What does the flag of Martinique look like?

It has a red triangle on the left, with the right side divided into a green upper section and a black lower section.

What colours are on the Martinique flag?

The flag uses three main colours: red, green and black.

When was the current Martinique flag adopted?

The FlagAtlas Martinique colouring resource states that the red, green and black flag was adopted in 2023.

Is Martinique part of France?

Yes. Martinique is a French overseas territory in the Caribbean and uses the euro as its currency. Martinique's tourism authority describes it as a French Overseas Territorial Collectivity. citeturn0search24

Why is Martinique sometimes called the Island of Flowers?

Martinique is widely known as the Island of Flowers. Its tourism authority uses this name and highlights the island's rich plant and animal life. citeturn0search26turn0search10
