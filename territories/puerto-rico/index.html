Flag of Puerto Rico

Five red and white stripes, a blue triangle and a single white star — the flag of Puerto Rico.

The flag of Puerto Rico has five horizontal stripes — three red and two white — with a blue triangle at the hoist carrying a single white five-pointed star. Puerto Rico is an unincorporated territory of the United States in the Caribbean, and its people are US citizens. The flag is flown with great pride across the island and by the Puerto Rican diaspora around the world.

About the Puerto Rico flag

The design dates back to 1895 and was officially adopted in 1952. The single white star — the “lone star” — represents the island itself. The three red stripes are often said to stand for the three branches of government, the two white stripes for the liberty and rights of the people, and the blue triangle for the sky and the seas around the island. The flag is almost identical to Cuba's, with the red and blue reversed, reflecting the shared history of the two islands and their independence movements in the late nineteenth century.
