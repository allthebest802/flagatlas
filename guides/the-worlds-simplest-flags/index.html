The World's Simplest Flags

Some flags are remarkably simple. A few can be recognised from only two colours and a basic shape — yet their designs can carry centuries of history.

FlagAtlas Guides · Updated August 2026

The short answer:
Some flags are remarkably simple. A few can be recognised from only two colours and a basic shape — yet their designs can carry centuries of history.

Japan

Japan's flag is a white field with a red disc representing the sun. Its simplicity makes it one of the world's most recognisable national flags.

Poland

Poland uses two horizontal bands: white above red. The design is extremely simple, with no emblem or complicated artwork.

Indonesia and Monaco

Both countries use a simple red-over-white design. Their flags show how a pair of horizontal colour fields can create a distinctive national symbol.

Switzerland

Switzerland combines a red field with a simple white cross. It is visually uncomplicated but immediately recognisable, and its unusual square shape makes it stand out.

Why simple flags work

Simple designs are easy to recognise at a distance, easy to reproduce and easy for children to draw. Good flag design often depends on being memorable rather than complicated.
